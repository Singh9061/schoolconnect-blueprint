# SchoolConnect Blueprint

Act as an expert UI/UX designer and web developer specializing in educational institutions. Create a comprehensive, user-friendly, and modern website blueprint and complete workflow for "Modern Public School, Pratapgarh". 

The website must cater to three primary user groups: prospective parents/students, current parents/students, and school administration. 

### 1. Brand Identity & Design System:

- School Name: Modern Public School, Pratapgarh

- Vibe: Professional, trustworthy, nurturing, and modern.

- Visuals: Clean layout, high-readability typography, and intuitive navigation anchors.

### 2. Website Structure & Page Workflows:

#### Navigation Menu (Header):

[Home] | [About Us] | [Academics] | [Admissions] | [Facilities] | [Student Life] | [Contact Us] | [Parent Portal Login]

#### Page-by-Page Workflow & Content Layout:

1. HOME PAGE:

- Hero Section: High-quality image slider of the campus/classroom. Headline: "Nurturing Excellence, Inspiring Futures at Modern Public School, Pratapgarh."

- Quick Links Call-to-Action (CTA): [Apply Online], [Pay Fees], [Academic Calendar].

- Director/Principal’s Welcome Note: A brief welcoming message focusing on holistic growth and quality education.

- Core Highlights: 3 columns showing "Academic Excellence", "State-of-the-Art Facilities", and "Holistic Development".

- Latest News & Notice Board: Dynamic section for upcoming exams, holidays, and event announcements.

2. ABOUT US PAGE:

- School Overview: Profile highlighting its dedication to providing a well-rounded education in Chhatarur, Pratapgarh.

- Vision & Mission Statement: Focusing on building self-confidence, academic brilliance, discipline, and moral values.

- Leadership & Faculty: Profiles of the Director, Manager, Principal, and teaching staff.

3. ACADEMICS PAGE:

- Curriculum: Detail the schooling levels (Primary & Upper Primary sections) following standard educational frameworks.

- Subjects & Medium of Instruction: Emphasize a strong academic curriculum alongside English/Hindi medium instruction details.

- Examination & Grading System: Details on regular evaluations, report card distribution, and feedback workflows.

4. ADMISSIONS PAGE:

- Step-by-Step Workflow: 

  Step 1: Read online guidelines & eligibility.

  Step 2: Click the "Proceed to Online Registration Form" button.

  Step 3: Document Upload (Birth certificate, previous report cards).

  Step 4: Interactive verification schedule & admission confirmation.

- Fee Structure: Clear, transparent breakdown of tuition and transport fees.

5. FACILITIES PAGE:

- Infrastructure Grid: Detail the recreation playgrounds, well-equipped classrooms, medical support/first-aid rooms, science labs, and library.

- Transport Route Module: Information on school bus routes across Pratapgarh with an allocation request map interface.

6. CONTACT US PAGE:

- Interactive Inquiry Form: Fields for Parent Name, Phone Number, Email, Student Grade Interest, and Custom Query message.

- School Info Footer: Modern Public School, Chhatarur, Pratapgarh, Uttar Pradesh. Include fields for dedicated phone numbers, email, and an embedded map location.

### 3. Core Functional Workflows (Backend/ERP Integrations):

- Parent & Staff Portal Access: Dedicated login gateway for the "SStudy iOS / Android App" to access individual student ledgers, daily homework tracking, and attendance logs.

- Notification Workflow: Automated WhatsApp/SMS alerts triggered for critical updates like exam results and pending fee balances.

Please generate the complete HTML structural map, UI layout suggestions, and copy for the sections above.
